⚡ 2. Ephemeral Processing & Zero-Retention Policy

Zero Storage Guarantee: Your photos remain exclusively yours. We never permanently save, store, sell, or share your images.

When you select a photo to enhance, restore, or transform using Lumina AI:

  • The image is transmitted securely over an encrypted HTTPS TLS 1.3 connection.
  • The photo is loaded into temporary volatile memory (RAM) solely for the seconds required to compute the neural AI enhancement.
  • Immediately upon returning the processed output to your device, the image data is permanently purged from memory.
  • Your photos are NEVER used to train public machine learning models.

📂 3. Device Permissions & Data Usage

Lumina requests only the minimum necessary permissions to function:

  • Photo Library / Storage Access: Required strictly to allow you to import images you wish to edit and save enhanced creations back to your device.
  • Camera Access (Optional): Required only if you choose to capture a new photo directly within the app.
  • Internet Access: Required to communicate with our private, GPU-accelerated cloud nodes for real-time AI generation.

We do not access location data, contacts, device identifiers, or any background telemetry.

  • You can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
