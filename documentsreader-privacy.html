01 Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Documents Reader & PDF Editor (the "Application", "App", or "Service"), developed and operated by Coregrit Studio ("we", "us", "our").

We are deeply committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of our users. This policy explains what information the application accesses, how it is processed, why specific device permissions are required, and what choices and rights you have regarding your data.

By installing and using Documents Reader & PDF Editor, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the application.

02 Local Document Processing (Privacy By Design)

Zero Server Upload Architecture All document viewing, rendering, word processing, spreadsheet calculations, presentation slides, PDF annotations, digital signatures, image conversions, and scanner operations are conducted 100% locally on your device hardware.

Our core application architecture is built from the ground up on privacy-preserving principles:

  • No Cloud Document Storage: We do not operate remote servers to store, index, read, or analyze your files.
  • No Content Inspection: We cannot read the contents of your PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets, slides, photos, or text files.
  • Safe Export & Sharing: When you share an exported PDF or document, it is transferred exclusively through your device's native operating system share sheet according to your explicit selection.

03 Permissions & Why We Need Them

To provide all-in-one document reader and office suite functionality, the application requests the following Android permissions:

Permission Android Manifest Name Purpose & Usage
All Files Access / Storage MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE		 Core Functionality: Required to scan your device storage for PDF, DOC/DOCX, XLS/XLSX, PPT/PPTX, and TXT files, display them in organized categories, allow editing/annotation, and save modified files.
Camera android.permission.CAMERA Document Scanner: Optional. Used only when you open the built-in Document Scanner to capture physical pages and convert them into multi-page PDF documents.
Photo / Media Library READ_MEDIA_IMAGES Image to PDF: Optional. Used solely when you choose images from your gallery to assemble into a PDF document.
Internet Access android.permission.INTERNET Ad Delivery & Updates: Used exclusively by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to serve advertisements and check for app updates.
Google Play All Files Access Policy Compliance: The MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE permission is strictly utilized for the app's primary purpose as a universal Document Management and File Reader suite. The app does not access media or files unrelated to supported document formats.

05 Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Regardless of your geographic location, we uphold robust data privacy standards:

Under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right of Access: You have the right to know what personal data is processed.
  • Right to Erasure / Deletion: You can erase all cached app data instantly by clearing the app's cache/storage in your device Settings or uninstalling the app.
  • Consent Management: Users in the EEA and UK are presented with consent dialogs to choose personalized vs. non-personalized advertising.

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA):

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising without consent.
  • You can reset or opt-out of personalized ads at any time via your Android Settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset or Delete Advertising ID.

06 Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our application is not intended for or directed toward children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain European jurisdictions). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal identifiable information from children.

If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided personal information or interacted inappropriately with our service, please contact us immediately at documentsreader@coregritstudio.com so we can take necessary corrective action.

07 Data Security & Retention

We implement stringent technical measures to safeguard user data:

  • No Server-Side Data Retention: Because we do not upload your documents or store personal accounts, there is no centralized database vulnerable to data breaches.
  • Device Sandbox: All application caches and temporary rendering buffers reside within Android's secured application sandbox.
  • Immediate Local Cleanup: Temporary files generated during image conversions or scanning are purged promptly.
